# Glitch

## Player Controls
- Move left and right
- Jump and double jump
- Perform a basic attack
- Dodge roll (unlockable)
- Pause and resume gameplay

## Core Mechanics
- Track health, stamina, and energy resources.
- Collect gems to purchase upgrades for attack speed, damage, and maximum health.
- Gain experience by defeating enemies to level up.
- Defeating each boss grants a unique Glitch Skill.

## Glitch Skills
1. **Echo Warp** – leave a damaging afterimage trail.
2. **Phase Break** – slow time for 2 seconds.
3. **Fractal Dash** – perform a short-range teleport that explodes on arrival.
4. **Static Storm** – emit pulsing electric fields.
5. **Reality Shift** – reverse gravity for 3 seconds.

## Progression
- Earn gems and experience by eliminating enemies.
- The game auto-saves level progress and unlocked skills.
- Player data persists across sessions through account binding.
- Upon defeat, the player respawns at the most recently unlocked checkpoint.
